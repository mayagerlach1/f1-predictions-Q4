Treatments

  • One seat, three drivers. The second Haas was driven by Ocon (rounds 1–18), Hirakawa (19) and Câmara (20). Earlier versions of the project stored all three under one code; here each result uses the real driver.
  • Team changes. Lawson raced for Red Bull in rounds 12–14, with Tsunoda at Racing Bulls and Hadjar absent. The team column stores the team for that race, not the driver's usual team.
  • Retirements have an empty finish position and status DNF/NC, rather than being given a fake position.
  • Disqualifications (Las Vegas 2025, McLaren) are kept as records with status DSQ (technical) instead of being deleted or counted as crashes.
  • Missing grids and poles are left empty, not estimated. A pole is not filled from grid P1, because penalties can change the starting order.
  • Track history is filtered to drivers on the 2026 grid.

What the data supports

  • Time comparison (H1 rounds 1–10 vs H2 rounds 11–20): Red Bull rose from 14.5 to 25.0 race points per race; Mercedes eased from 31.1 to 27.6.
  • Segment 1, starting position: drivers starting P1–3 finished 4.1th on average (8% retired); P4–10 finished 7.3th (13%); P11–22 finished 12.7th (24%). Based on the 12 races with grids.
  • Segment 2, conditions: wet races had a lower retirement rate (9% of 44 starts) than dry races (20% of 396). Only 2 wet races, so this is weak evidence, but it contradicts the model's assumption that rain raises retirements.
  • Other segments available: team, driver, sprint vs non-sprint weekend.
  • Record level: every result can be traced to a race, driver and team in results.csv.

Gaps and scope adjustments

  • No fantasy prices or official fantasy points. The brief's "worth their price" question can't be answered yet. Adjustment: use championship race points and retirement risk as a proxy for fantasy value, and state this in the app.
  • Grids for 12 of 20 races. Any analysis by starting position uses those 12 races only.
  • Sprint order beyond 8th not collected. Fine for points; not usable for sprint position-change analysis.
  • Few wet races. Wet findings are indicative only.
